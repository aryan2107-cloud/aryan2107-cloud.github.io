Recorded for Prof. Bruce Maxwell's CS5330 (Pattern Recognition and Computer Vision) at Northeastern. The goal was to walk through the Transformer architecture from Vaswani et al. (2017) end-to-end, then show how Dosovitskiy et al. (2021) ported the whole thing to images with the Vision Transformer.
